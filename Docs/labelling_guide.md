# CivicTriage: Ground-Truth Labelling Guide

This guide establishes the standardized criteria for classifying civic grievances across languages (English, Hindi, Telugu) into priority levels, emotion categories, and civic service domains.

---

## 1. Priority Levels

Priority is determined by **immediate threat to life/safety, health hazards, degree of disruption to essential services, and duration of suffering**.

### A. Immediate (High Urgency)
*Action Required: Same-day emergency response or within hours.*
- **Criteria**:
  - Direct, immediate hazard to human life or bodily safety (e.g., exposed live electrical wire, sparking transformer, collapse of road/bridge, uncovered manhole on active road).
  - Severe flooding entering homes, toxic sewage backflow into living spaces, contaminated drinking water causing active illness.
  - Critical public safety threats (e.g., gas leaks, violent encroachment blocking emergency vehicles).
- **Representative Examples**:
  - *English*: "A high-voltage power cable has snapped and is lying in a waterlogged street outside our house! Children are walking by!"
  - *Hindi*: "गली के मोड़ पर बिजली का खंभा गिर गया है और नंगे तार में करंट दौड़ रहा है। जान का खतरा है, तुरंत ठीक करें।"
  - *Telugu*: "మా వీధిలో మెయిన్ రోడ్డుపై మ్యాన్‌హోల్ మూత విరిగిపోయి తెరిచి ఉంది. రాత్రిపూట బైకులు పడిపోయే ప్రమాదం ఉంది."

### B. Soon (Medium Urgency)
*Action Required: 24 to 72 hours response.*
- **Criteria**:
  - Essential service completely disrupted but no immediate life-threatening danger (e.g., no municipal tap water supply for 3+ consecutive days).
  - Blocked main drainage causing large stagnant pools without active home flooding, overflowing commercial garbage heaps causing severe stench and pest breeding.
  - Deep dangerous potholes damaging vehicles, dead streetlights across an entire residential block causing safety anxieties at night.
- **Representative Examples**:
  - *English*: "No drinking water supplied to our ward for the last 4 days. Tankers are not arriving and families are suffering."
  - *Hindi*: "पिछले 5 दिनों से इलाके में सफाई कर्मचारी नहीं आए हैं। कचरे के ढेर से बदबू फैल रही है और मच्छर पैदा हो रहे हैं।"
  - *Telugu*: "నాలుగు రోజులుగా మా కాలనీలో మంచినీటి సరఫరా పూర్తిగా నిలిచిపోయింది. వంటకు, తాగడానికి నీరు లేక ఇబ్బంది పడుతున్నాం."

### C. Routine (Normal Urgency)
*Action Required: Scheduled municipal maintenance (3 to 7+ days).*
- **Criteria**:
  - Minor infrastructure wear and tear, cosmetic damage, or maintenance requests that do not impede daily safety or access.
  - Routine requests: street tree branch trimming, minor road resurfacing/patchwork, replacement of a single flickering bulb, general inquiries or non-critical encroachment reports.
- **Representative Examples**:
  - *English*: "The speed breaker paint near the park entrance has faded away. Please repaint it during routine maintenance."
  - *Hindi*: "पार्क के पास एक स्ट्रीट लाइट की ट्यूबलाइट धीमी जल रही है। जब समय मिले कृपया बदलवा दें।"
  - *Telugu*: "మా ఇంటి ముందున్న వీధి దీపం ఒక వారం నుండి వెలగడం లేదు, వీలైనప్పుడు మార్చగలరు."

## 2. Emotion Categories

Classify the citizen's dominant emotional tone reflected in the grievance text:

| Emotion | Definition & Indicators | Example Phrasing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Angry** | Citizen expresses explicit outrage, indignation, harsh criticism of municipal negligence, threats of escalation or protest. | "Incompetent staff! We pay taxes for what?", "लापरवाही की हद है!", "ఇంత నిర్లక్ష్యమా! ధర్నా చేస్తాం!" |
| **Anxious** | Citizen communicates fear, apprehension regarding impending danger, safety of children/elderly, or disease outbreak. | "I am terrified someone will get electrocuted", "डर लग रहा है कोई बड़ा हादसा न हो जाए", "పిల్లలు భయపడుతున్నారు" |
| **Sad** | Citizen communicates helplessness, grief, exhaustion, or distress over prolonged deprivation or suffering. | "We are helpless and begging for help", "हम बहुत परेशान और लाचार हैं", "దిక్కుతోచని స్థితిలో ఉన్నాం" |
| **Neutral** | Matter-of-fact, objective, polite, or informational reporting without strong emotional charge. | "Street light pole #14 is non-functional near sector 3", "कृपया नाली की सफाई करवाएं", "రహదారిపై చిన్న గుంత ఉంది" |

## 3. Civic Categories

Assign exactly one primary category based on the municipal department responsible:
1. **Water**: Drinking water shortage, pipe leaks, low pressure, contaminated water, tanker delivery issues.
2. **Roads**: Potholes, damaged tarmac, missing speed breakers, caved-in roads, broken footpaths.
3. **Sanitation**: Garbage collection failure, clogged drains, sewage overflows, public toilet hygiene, open defecation.
4. **Electricity**: Snapped wires, sparking transformers, non-functional streetlights, tilted electricity poles.
5. **Other**: Public park maintenance, noise complaints, illegal banners, stray animal menace, encroachment.
